## Source and behavior

The release preserves `energy = free_energy = E_KohnSham`. It exposes
`energy_without_entropy = E_KohnSham - E_entropy(-TS)` in parsed `Atoms.info`
and file-backed calculator results when the entropy term is printed. Missing
entropy is not fabricated. This lets ATST reproduce FCP v2's reference-only
energy convention while keeping ordinary ASE and compensated-gate energies
force-consistent. Socket behavior is unchanged.

## Independent review and correction

Review of `e8acc4c..8131151` and ATST `e427a70..9def2c6` found two Important
issues before merge: the candidate mislabeled the no-entropy value as ASE
`free_energy`, and ATST swapped energy keys for both CP boundaries. ABACUS
`source/source_estate/fp_energy.cpp` adds `demet` to `etot`, and
`elecstate_print.cpp` prints `E_KohnSham=etot` and `E_entropy(-TS)=demet`.
The legacy engine uses the same convention. The repair preserves those
standard energies and limits the extra metadata's use to `reference_fcp`.

Regression evidence: five parser/handoff failures and two CP-boundary failures
were observed before the repair; the parser/handoff set then passed 73 tests.
Coverage includes both MD formats, matching-frame SCF selection, missing
entropy and different energy values under each CP boundary. Final full-suite,
installed-distribution, reviewer and hosted evidence is recorded below.
